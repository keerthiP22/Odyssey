# Overview

Mission Control is the central workspace of Odyssey.

Rather than overwhelming users with information, it provides a personalized daily briefing that combines goals, learning, reflections, and AI guidance into one focused experience.

Mission Control serves as the starting point of every user's day and the place they return to review progress, reflect, and prepare for what comes next.

---

# Vision

Mission Control should feel like opening a conversation with a trusted mentor instead of opening a productivity dashboard.

Every element should answer one of three questions:

- What matters today?
- Why does it matter?
- What should I do next?

---

# Objectives

Mission Control should:

- Provide clarity.
- Reduce decision fatigue.
- Encourage consistency.
- Surface meaningful insights.
- Promote intentional reflection.
- Keep users focused on long-term growth.

---

# Responsibilities

Mission Control is responsible for presenting:

- Morning Briefing
- Daily Mission
- Goal Progress
- Learning Focus
- AI Insights
- Reflection Prompt
- Growth Streaks
- Weekly Highlights
- Quick Actions

Mission Control does **not** generate recommendations or perform AI reasoning.

It displays information generated by other systems.

---

# Daily Experience

Every day begins with a personalized briefing.

Example:

> Good morning, Keerthi.

Today's mission is to complete your Machine Learning lesson and spend 20 minutes practicing DSA.

Yesterday you completed 3 learning sessions and maintained your 8-day streak.

Reflection prompt:

"What is one thing you learned yesterday that changed your perspective?"

---

# Core Components

## 1. Morning Briefing

Displays:

- Greeting
- Weather (optional)
- Daily encouragement
- AI summary
- Focus for the day

---

## 2. Today's Mission

Displays:

- Highest priority goal
- Recommended learning session
- Suggested habits
- Estimated effort
- Completion progress

---

## 3. AI Insight

Displays one personalized observation.

Examples:

- Your consistency has improved this week.
- You learn better during the morning.
- You finish more tasks after journaling.
- Taking breaks every 90 minutes improves your focus.

Insights should always be explainable.

---

## 4. Learning Focus

Displays:

- Today's lesson
- Practice challenge
- Revision recommendation
- Learning streak

---

## 5. Reflection

Displays:

- Reflection prompt
- Journal shortcut
- Mood check-in
- Weekly review reminder

Reflection is optional but encouraged.

---

## 6. Goal Progress

Displays:

- Active goals
- Milestone completion
- Weekly progress
- Remaining milestones

---

## 7. Growth Insights

Displays:

- Learning consistency
- Goal completion rate
- Reflection streak
- Skill growth
- Weekly improvements

---

## 8. Quick Actions

Provides shortcuts for:

- Start Learning
- Open Journal
- Create Goal
- Ask AI Coach
- Review Growth Map

# Design Principles

Mission Control should be:

- Calm
- Focused
- Minimal
- Personalized
- Explainable
- Action-oriented

Information should be prioritized rather than maximized.

---

# Functional Requirements

Mission Control shall:

- Display personalized daily briefings.
- Surface active goals.
- Show learning recommendations.
- Display AI insights.
- Provide reflection prompts.
- Present weekly highlights.
- Offer quick navigation to major systems.

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

Mission Control must:

- Load quickly.
- Adapt to different screen sizes.
- Support accessibility.
- Explain AI-generated insights.
- Update dynamically throughout the day.

---

# Success Metrics

Mission Control is successful if users:

- Start their day from Mission Control.
- Complete recommended missions.
- Engage with reflections.
- Return multiple times per day.
- Feel clear about their priorities.

# Summary

Mission Control is the heart of Odyssey.

It transforms information from every major system into one intelligent, personalized workspace that helps users begin each day with clarity, maintain focus throughout the day, and end it with meaningful reflection.

Rather than acting as a dashboard, Mission Control serves as a daily guide for continuous personal growth.
