# 🌙 Odyssey Home Experience

Version: MVP 1
Sprint: 4
Status: Designing

---

# Purpose

The Home experience is the heart of Odyssey.

It is not a dashboard.

It is the user's first interaction with Odyssey every day.

Its purpose is to reduce mental load, help users begin their day intentionally, and allow the AI to gradually understand them better.

---

# Design Principles

The Home experience should always feel:

- Calm
- Safe
- Personal
- Intelligent
- Minimal

Never:

- Overwhelming
- Corporate
- Dashboard-like
- Data-heavy

---

# Home Philosophy

Instead of asking

"What do you want to do today?"

Odyssey should say

"I've already prepared today for you."

The AI reduces thinking before asking the user to make decisions.

---

# Morning State

Purpose

Help the user transition into the day.

Components

1. Greeting
2. Manifestation
3. Morning Routine
4. AI Recommendation
5. Today's Focus
6. Tiny Moments
7. Brain Dump
8. Quick Actions

# Greeting

Purpose

Welcome the user.

Display

- Good Morning
- User name
- Date

---

AI learns

Nothing.

---

# Manifestation

Purpose

Remind users who they are becoming.

Displays

- User manifestation

---

# Morning Routine

Purpose

Help the user prepare physically before beginning work.

Default Items

- Drink Water
- Freshen Up
- Stretch
- Breakfast
- Make Bed

Customizable

Yes.

AI learns

- Morning consistency
- Preferred routine

# AI Recommendation

# Day 1 Experience

Odyssey does not pretend to know the user.

Instead

Ask one simple question each day.

Examples

"What are you working toward this year?"

"What usually distracts you?"

"What helps you focus?"

The AI slowly builds user understanding.

---

# Constraints

Maximum 6 visible sections.

Maximum 3 recommendations.

One primary focus.

No analytics.

No streaks.

No gamification.

No large calendar.

No social feed.
