# 1. Executive Summary

Odyssey is an AI-powered Personal Growth Operating System designed to help individuals achieve meaningful long-term goals through adaptive coaching, intelligent planning, personalized learning, and continuous reflection.

Unlike traditional productivity applications that focus on managing tasks, Odyssey focuses on helping users become the person they aspire to be.

The platform combines artificial intelligence, behavioral insights, learning systems, and visual planning into one unified experience.

---

# 2. Product Vision

Enable users to consistently achieve long-term goals through an intelligent AI companion that adapts to their journey.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Current productivity and learning tools have several limitations:

- They focus on tasks instead of outcomes.
- They do not adapt to changing circumstances.
- Learning lacks context and personalization.
- Motivation decreases over time.
- Users often abandon goals due to poor planning and lack of guidance.

# 7. Functional Requirements

The system shall:

- Allow users to create goals.
- Generate personalized roadmaps.
- Recommend daily tasks.
- Adapt recommendations based on progress.
- Provide AI coaching.
- Track learning.
- Visualize skill progression.
- Generate reflections.
- Provide analytics.

# 10. Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following will not be included in the initial release:

- Team collaboration
- Marketplace
- Social networking
- Wearable integrations
- Voice assistant
- Enterprise features

These may be considered in future versions.
